## The Hawkstone Brewing Philosophy

### Premium Ingredients

![Premium barley used in Hawkstone brewery tours](https://www.ukbrewerytours.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Premium-barley-used-in-Hawkstone-brewery-tours.jpeg)

We use only the highest quality malted barley that passes our stringent quality tests. This notoriously temperamental crop requires perfect conditions to produce the exceptional lager you'll taste on our tours.

### Traditional Lagering

![Traditional lagering process featured in Hawkstone brewery tours](https://www.ukbrewerytours.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Traditional-lagering-process-featured-in-Hawkstone-brewery-tours.jpeg)

Our brewing process includes a longer maturing process called lagering – similar to laying down a fine wine. This patience produces smoother, more rounded flavors that you'll experience firsthand during your tour.

### Craft & Expertise

![Craft and expertise highlighted during Hawkstone brewery tours](https://www.ukbrewerytours.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Craft-and-expertise-highlighted-during-Hawkstone-brewery-tours.jpeg)

The perfect blend of barley, belligerence, and outright brewing skill goes into every batch. Our expert brewers combine traditional methods with modern techniques to create our distinctive flavor profiles. Booking a tour is also a hands-on way of [supporting independent breweries](/blog/drink-local-independent-breweries/) that put craft before scale.
