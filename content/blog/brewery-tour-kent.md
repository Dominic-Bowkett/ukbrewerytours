## Discover Kent's Finest Breweries

Kent boasts a rich history of brewing, and its breweries are among the finest in the UK, standing comfortably alongside [the UK's best beer destinations](/blog/best-uk-beer-cities-2026/). From traditional ale houses to contemporary microbreweries, there is a diverse range of establishments to discover on a brewery tour of Kent.

### Shepherd Neame Brewery

Founded in 1698, Shepherd Neame is Britain's oldest brewer. Its picturesque brewery in Faversham offers an immersive tour where you can learn about the company's long history, see the brewing process in action, and taste some of its most popular beers. Don't miss the chance to visit the Old Brewery Store, a unique gift shop housed in a beautiful Victorian building.

### Whitstable Brewery

Located in the charming seaside town of Whitstable, the brewery produces a range of award-winning cask and bottled beers. Its welcoming taproom is the perfect spot to sample its offerings while basking in the beautiful coastal views. Take a guided tour to learn about the brewing process and get an insight into the brewery's dedication to sustainability.

### Old Dairy Brewery

The Old Dairy Brewery is a small, family-run microbrewery in the village of Tenterden. It has won a string of awards for its contemporary take on traditional English beer styles. Visit its taproom to try its ever-changing lineup of fresh brews and discover what sets it apart from other breweries in Kent.

### Canterbury Brewers and Distillers

Canterbury Brewers and Distillers produces a wide range of craft beers and spirits from its base in the heart of the city. Its taproom is open seven days a week, offering a friendly and relaxed atmosphere where you can sample its core range and limited edition brews. Don't forget to check out the distillery, where its award-winning gin is made.

### Goody Ales

Goody Ales is a small, independent brewery based in the village of Herne. It champions locally sourced ingredients and produces a range of organic beers, including some innovative fruit-infused brews. Book a beer and cheese tasting experience to explore the unique flavour combinations created by its expert team.
