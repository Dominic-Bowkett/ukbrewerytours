## Bermondsey Beer Mile (https://www.ukbrewerytours.com/bermondsey-beer-mile/)

### Overview
UK Brewery Tours was founded in 2014 in Bermondsey and runs weekly tours on the Bermondsey Beer Mile in Southwark. Private tours available by request.

### Tours & pricing (all Saturdays; gift vouchers available for all)
- **Bermondsey Cheese Meat & Beer Tour — £65.00**
- **Bermondsey Microbrewery Experience — £54.00**
- **Bermondsey Beer Tasting Tour — £49.00**

### Historical background (as stated on page)
- The area was home to the **Barclay Perkins Brewery in 1809** — the world's largest brewery at that time, famous for Porter.
- The modern craft renaissance began in **2009 when Evin O'Riordain founded The Kernel Brewery**.
- Subsequent openings: Partizan Brewery, Brew by Numbers, Anspach and Hobday, Fourpure, Southwark Brewery.
