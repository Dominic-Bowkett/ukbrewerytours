The small print

Returns policy

Gift voucher returns

Gift vouchers are unique custom made items and therefore we do not allow returns on either the digital or postal copies.

Refunds

Customers have a 24-hour window following purchase to request a refund. Such refunds are processed back to the original card within 28 days.

Postal vouchers

Delivery and packaging expenses are non-refundable once the postal voucher has been dispatched.

Should a postal voucher arrive damaged in transit, a replacement copy will be sent at no additional cost on one occasion only.
