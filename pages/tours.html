Brewery tours explained

Choosing a brewery tour or beer tasting in the UK

Britain has thousands of breweries, from Victorian family firms to railway-arch microbreweries, and visiting them is one of the best ways to spend an afternoon. The tours on this page fall into three groups.

Guided brewery tours and beer walks

Our own tours take a small group to several breweries and taprooms in one city, such as the Bermondsey Beer Mile in London, Bristol's harbourside or the Northern Quarter in Manchester. A guide explains how each beer is brewed and what to look for in the glass. The beer is included, usually in third- or half-pint measures, so you can try a range of styles from pale ales to stouts.

Beer tasting experiences

A beer tasting is a seated, tutored session, often in one venue, with a host taking you through a flight of beers or food pairings. They're shorter and more relaxed, and a good choice if walking between venues isn't for you.

Tours run by the breweries themselves

Many breweries, including Fuller's, Harvey's and Hook Norton, run tours of their own brewhouse. We list these as "Book direct", and you book them straight with the brewery.

How much does a brewery tour cost?

Our tours cost from £17 to £69 per person, with most between £38 and £50. Tours last from under an hour to around four hours, depending on how many venues you visit. Prices and lengths are shown on every card above.
