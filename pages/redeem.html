Good to know

Voucher questions, answered

What if the tour costs more than my voucher?

No problem — you can top up the difference. Just send your request as normal and we'll email you a secure payment link for the balance.

What happens to leftover credit?

It stays on your voucher code. Remaining credit can be used on further tours in the future — our vouchers never expire.

Not sure what to do?

Just email us or message us on WhatsApp and we'll sort everything for you — be sure to include your voucher code.

Where do I see tour dates?

Each tour's page on this website shows its upcoming dates — start from the tours page and pick your city.
