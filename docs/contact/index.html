FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is beer included in the ticket price?

Yes — we include the beer with the ticket. You'll generally taste 1/3 or 1/2 pint measures so that you can taste and learn about a variety of different beer styles, from pale ales to stouts.

Are other drinks available?

Wine and spirits are available at extra cost, and non-alcoholic options can be arranged — just let us know when booking.

Do gift vouchers expire?

No — our monetary gift vouchers never expire. They're delivered instantly by email and can be redeemed against any of our brewery tours and beer tastings whenever suits.

Are tours wheelchair accessible?

Routes can be adapted where possible. Get in touch before booking and we'll confirm what's practical for the tour you're interested in.

Are tours available in languages other than English?

All tours are conducted in English. You would need to provide a translator if required.

Can we wear fancy dress?

Unfortunately not. We like to focus on the beer tasting, and many venues don't allow fancy dress — sorry!

Can I bring my dog?

It depends on the city and the venues visited — ask us when booking and we'll check for you.
