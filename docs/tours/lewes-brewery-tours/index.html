About this tour

Enjoy a brewery tour and tasting in Lewes.

Lewes is a charming town located in East Sussex, England. It is known for its picturesque streets, historic landmarks, and cultural significance. The town is surrounded by the South Downs National Park, which provides a stunning backdrop for outdoor activities such as hiking and cycling. Lewes is also home to some of the finest breweries in the country, where visitors can learn about the brewing process, sample some delicious beers, and immerse themselves in the town's rich brewing heritage. Whether you're a history buff, an outdoor enthusiast, or a beer lover, Lewes has something to offer for everyone.

Your guide

That's The Beer Talking is founded by Dan, who's a Certified Cicerone & International Beer Judge, and will be on hand to tell you everything about Lewes & Beer that you never knew you needed to know, while drinking a variety of the freshest, hand picked beers we can get our hands on.

What you'll do

Visit Harvey's Brewery, Abyss Brewing Co. & Beak Brewery on this monthly beer tour with tastings included.
