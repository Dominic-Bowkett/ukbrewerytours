About this tour

Glasgow's craft beer scene has flourished over the past decade, and this relaxed, hour-long beer tasting puts it front and centre. From long-established institutions to a new wave of independent breweries, the city has become one of the UK's most rewarding places to drink good beer. Set inside Platform, one of Glasgow's most iconic food-and-drink venues, our expert-led session is a brilliant introduction to the styles, stories and flavours behind great craft beer. Whether you're marking a special occasion or simply fancy learning something new while you sip, it's one of the most sociable ways to spend an hour in the city.

What you'll do

This is a tutored beer tasting hosted at Platform in the heart of Glasgow, led by one of our knowledgeable local guides. No prior knowledge is needed — you'll be guided through everything as you go. Over 60 minutes you'll work through a selection of classic beer styles, discovering how to taste properly and pick out the characteristics that make each one distinctive. Your guide weaves in the history of beer and the story of how it's brewed, so you come away understanding not just what you're drinking but how and why it ends up in your glass. Expect a friendly, low-pressure atmosphere where the emphasis falls on enjoying yourself as much as learning something new.

It's designed to be interactive rather than a lecture. You'll get hands-on with the raw ingredients that go into beer — sniffing and handling things like malt and hops — and explore how they shape its aroma and flavour, with plenty of room for questions along the way. As you compare styles side by side, you'll start to notice how ingredients, brewing methods and history all combine in the beer in front of you. Warm, informative and thoroughly easy-going, the session is a great fit for curious newcomers and committed beer lovers alike.
