About this tour

Discover the best that Cardiff has to offer at a number of different craft beer venues in the city. This is available as a private group tour for a minimum of 10 people.

Our Cardiff Craft Beer Tour allows guests to sample quality, independent Welsh craft beers at a selection of Cardiff's specialist craft beer bars and tap rooms including a local brewery. We sample different beer styles such as pale ales, IPAs and stouts. It's all about quality Welsh produce on this tour with beer from local suppliers. What could be better than a selection of quality independent craft beer?

What you'll do

This tour takes place inside 3 different Cardiff brewery taprooms and you'll enjoy beers at each stop. We gather within the walls of Cardiff's iconic city centre castle for meet-up and an introduction, before heading for a welcome drink at the city's newest brewery, a look behind the scenes at the brewery and an insight into how your beer is crafted. Your guide will be on hand to talk you through the carefully selected beers and explain how their ingredients and methods combine to the flavours that you'll enjoy.
