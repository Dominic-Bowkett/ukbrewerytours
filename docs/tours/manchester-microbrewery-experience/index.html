About this tour

During this Manchester Craft Beer Experience, you will visit several hidden craft beer gems that you might not have discovered otherwise. Each venue offers a unique atmosphere, and you'll learn all about the brewing process from our knowledgeable local beer guides.

At each stop, you'll have the opportunity to sample a wide variety of beer styles, handpicked by your guide to ensure you get a diverse and exciting tasting experience. From old favorites like IPAs to more unusual and rare beer styles like grisettes and wild-fermented beers, there's something for every palette and level of expertise.

Our group size is limited to 25 people to ensure everyone gets the attention they need and to make the tour more intimate and interactive. The walking distance is approximately 1 mile, so comfortable shoes are recommended.

What's included

  • Multiple brewery taproom visits
  • Behind-the-scenes tastings
  • Diverse beer style samples
  • Knowledgeable local guide
  • Limited group size (max 25 people)
