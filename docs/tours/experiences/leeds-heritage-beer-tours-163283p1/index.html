What this tour was

Get to know Leeds' historic beer industry on a small-group tour, limited to ten people, of some of the city's best-loved pubs. The tour begins at The Tetley arts hub, set in the iconic former Tetley brewery, and takes in the former flagship Adelphi, The Duck & Drake on Leeds' oldest street, the White Swan and the 300-year-old Whitelock's Ale House. Five half pints of locally brewed beer from highly rated breweries are included, alongside in-depth local knowledge from an experienced beer-industry guide. Along the way you'll hear about local history and walk some of the city's oldest streets. The experience lasts approximately three and a half hours and is suitable for adults aged 18 and over.
