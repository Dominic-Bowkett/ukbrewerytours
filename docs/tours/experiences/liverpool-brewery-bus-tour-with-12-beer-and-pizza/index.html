Liverpool: Brewery Bus Tour with 12 Beer and Pizza

4 hours★ 5.0 · 12 reviewsLiverpool

About this experience

Join a four-hour guided minibus tour visiting four local breweries around Liverpool. Along the way you sample between eight and twelve freshly crafted beers, paired with food including pizza. The tour offers behind-the-scenes access to the breweries and insights into the city's brewing scene, guided by a beer sommelier.

Highlights

  • Visit four local Liverpool breweries
  • Taste 8 to 12 freshly crafted beers
  • Enjoy food including pizza
  • Go behind the scenes at the breweries
  • Meet a beer sommelier
  • Comfortable minibus transport

Meeting point

Outside the Shankly Hotel building, by the main road.
