Edinburgh: Pub History Tour with Beer & Cider Tasting

2 hours★ 4.7 · 283 reviewsEdinburgh

About this experience

This guided two-hour walking tour explores Edinburgh's Old Town and its long brewing heritage, tracing how the city grew into a major centre for beer and cider production. Led by knowledgeable local guides, you visit historic and authentic pubs away from the usual tourist spots while hearing stories about the industry's rivalries, expansion and influence on the city. Along the way you taste a selection of award-winning Scottish beers and ciders and learn about the ingredients and science behind brewing.

Highlights

  • Two-hour guided walking tour of Old Town
  • Tastings of award-winning Scottish beers and cider
  • Visit authentic pubs beyond tourist areas
  • Expert local beer-enthusiast guide
  • Learn Edinburgh's brewing history and heritage
  • Insights into brewing ingredients and science

Meeting point

26 St Giles' Street, Old Town, Edinburgh (the last door at the end of the street next to the railings)
