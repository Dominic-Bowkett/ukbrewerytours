Brighton Bar Crawl: 5+ Venues, Free Shots, Free Club Entry

5 hours★ 4.5 · 12 reviewsLewes

About this experience

Join a guided evening bar crawl through Brighton's city centre, visiting at least five bars and clubs with energetic hosts leading the way. The experience is built around meeting new people and exploring the local nightlife, with a free shot at every bar and reduced drink prices via a wristband. The night finishes with free club entry.

  • Energetic hosts guiding the night
