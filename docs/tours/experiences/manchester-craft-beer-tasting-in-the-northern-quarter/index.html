Manchester: Craft Beer Tasting in the Northern Quarter

1.5 hours★ 5.0 · 2 reviewsManchester

About this experience

This 90-minute guided tasting masterclass explores Manchester's independent craft beer culture in the Northern Quarter. In a relaxed, seated venue with small groups of up to 10 people, you sample five modern craft beers while a knowledgeable host explains professional tasting techniques, brewing methods, ingredients and flavour profiles. It is designed for anyone curious about modern beer styles, from first-time tasters to seasoned enthusiasts.</description_md>
