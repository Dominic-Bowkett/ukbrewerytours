London: Small Group, Ancient Pubs and Historic Taverns Tour

2 hours★ 4.9 · 125 reviewsLondon

About this experience

This small-group guided walking tour leads you through some of London's most atmospheric pubs and historic taverns, several dating back to the 16th century. As you wind through cobbled streets, hidden courtyards and lesser-known corners of the city, your guide shares stories of the famous drinkers, criminals and local legends tied to each establishment. The experience combines historical storytelling with British ale tastings and an introduction to traditional pub culture. Participation in the drinking is entirely optional, so non-drinkers are equally welcome.</description_md>
