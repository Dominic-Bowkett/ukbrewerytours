Brighton: Local Beer Tasting Experience at a Micropub

1 hour★ 5.0 · 2 reviewsLewes

About this experience

Head to BeerBelly, an independent micropub and bottle shop in Brighton and Hove, just a short walk from the seafront. Meet Robin, the owner and beer enthusiast, for a tasting of a selection of local beers and craft brews from breweries across the UK. As you sip each one, you'll learn about the different flavours and styles and get to know the local beer scene. The experience finishes with the BeerBelly shot of the week.

Highlights

  • Taste local beers and UK craft brews
  • Tasting led by owner and beer enthusiast Robin
  • Visit a family-owned micropub and bottle shop
  • Learn about different beer flavours and styles
  • Finish with the BeerBelly shot of the week

Meeting point

BeerBelly micropub, Brighton and Hove, near the seafront
