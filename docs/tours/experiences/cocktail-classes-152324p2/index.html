About this experience

This private cocktail-making class gives your group the exclusive company of a mixologist in the centre of Bristol. Rather than watching someone else at work, you get behind the bar yourself to learn the secrets of mixing and shaking delicious cocktails. The two-hour session includes three cocktails each along with expert tips throughout. As it is a private booking with exclusive use of the venue, you can enjoy the experience without the crowds.
