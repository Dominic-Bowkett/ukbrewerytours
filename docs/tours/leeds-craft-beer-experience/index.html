About this tour

Leeds has quietly become one of the north's most rewarding cities for a craft beer tour, its railway arches, taprooms and independent breweries turning out everything from crisp lagers to hazy IPAs and rich, roasty stouts. This four-hour, expert-led walking tour is a proper deep dive into that scene — an in-depth guided beer tasting that begins with a behind-the-scenes brewery tour and works its way through five of the city's best-loved beer spots. Along the way you'll discover a range of beer styles and, just as importantly, the stories and the people behind them.

What you'll do

Nomadic Beers. The day starts here with a behind-the-scenes brewery tour of the equipment, so you can see exactly how the beer is made before you drink it — followed by your first tastings straight from the source.

Brew York Leeds. Next you'll head to Brew York's Leeds home to carry on sampling and comparing styles, with your guide talking you through each beer, its ingredients and how it's made.

North Brewing Co. A favourite of the city's contemporary craft scene, North Brewing Co is your next stop for more tastings and a closer look at how Leeds' independent brewers work.

Leeds City Tap. A relaxed taproom stop to keep exploring different styles, with expert guidance on how each beer is produced and what makes it distinctive.

Cross Keys. The tour finishes at the iconic Cross Keys with a final tasting — a fitting place to round off an afternoon of Leeds beer and reflect on the styles and stories you've discovered along the way.
