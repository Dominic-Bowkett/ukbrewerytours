About this tour

Liverpool's independent brewing scene has boomed over the past decade, with a new wave of microbreweries and taprooms turning the city into one of the North West's most exciting places for a pint. Areas like the Baltic Triangle have become magnets for independent brewers, small-batch beer and lively taprooms. This four-hour guided brewery tour takes you behind the scenes at some of the city's best independent breweries, all on a flat, easily walkable city-centre route. Part brewery tour and part beer tasting, it's a relaxed, sociable way to meet the people making the beer and to discover the styles that define Liverpool's craft scene.

What you'll do

This is a guided walking tour of four independent breweries and taprooms, linked by a flat, easy route through the heart of the city — no strenuous hiking required. Across the afternoon you'll enjoy ten craft beer tastings, going behind the scenes at working breweries to see how the beer is made and learning what sets each style apart. A local beer specialist guides you from stop to stop, on hand to talk you through what's in your glass and answer every question along the way.

Expect a mix of the city's established names and newer arrivals, taking in a range of beer styles across the afternoon. Your guide shares the stories behind Liverpool's breweries along with insider recommendations and plenty of beer-style education, championing the independent, inclusive venues that make the city's scene special. Whether you're a seasoned craft drinker or just starting to explore, it's a friendly, low-key introduction to the people and places driving Liverpool's beer. It's an easygoing afternoon built around good beer and good company.
