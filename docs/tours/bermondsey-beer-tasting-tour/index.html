About this tour

London's craft beer scene is among the most exciting anywhere in the world, and no corner of the city tells its story better than Bermondsey's famous 'beer mile' — a run of railway-arch breweries and taprooms threading through the heart of SE1. This tutored beer tasting tour walks you along the mile in expert company, turning a great afternoon out into a proper education in beer. It's a real step up from the usual crawl: accredited Beer Sommelier and Certified Cicerone® Paul Davies leads the way, with tutored tastings at four of the mile's best taprooms.

What you'll do

Your guide, accredited Beer Sommelier and Certified Cicerone® Paul Davies, hosts a tutored tasting at every stop — so you leave knowing not just which beers you enjoyed, but why. Along the way you'll pick up the sensory evaluation techniques that professional beer judges use to assess a glass, and hear the history and evolution of the styles you're tasting. This is tutored tasting rather than a rushed pub crawl, with time at each stop to smell, sip and talk through what makes each beer tick. Your four tasting stops are drawn from the mile's best-loved railway-arch breweries and taprooms, among them:

Southwark Brewing. The tour's meeting point and an established beer-mile favourite, where you'll settle in for your first tutored tasting.

Anspach and Hobday. A behind-the-arches taproom visit with a guided tasting of the styles brewed on site.

Moor Beer. Another characterful stop for tasting and expert commentary on what's in your glass.

Bianca Road. A modern craft brewery and taproom on the mile, with beers to sample and discuss.

Mash Paddle. A welcoming taproom stop for more tutored tasting along the route.

The Kernel. One of the names credited with sparking London's craft beer boom, and a fitting stop on any beer-mile tour.
