Eat & drink in Lancashire

Where to drink in Lancashire

Lancashire's beer story is one of stubborn independents. Moorhouse's has brewed in Burnley since 1865 and found fame with Pendle Witches Brew, named for the county's most notorious trial, while Thwaites, founded in Blackburn in 1807, remains one of the north's great family brewers.

Clitheroe is the modern surprise: Holmes Mill, a converted textile mill, houses Bowland Brewery's beer hall with a bar of heroic length and a food hall celebrating Lancashire produce. It's a fitting county for eating with your pint — think Lancashire hotpot, crumbly white Lancashire cheese and proper black pudding.

From Blackpool's seafront boozers to stone-flagged village inns in the Ribble Valley, this is a county that takes its drinking unfussily and seriously at once.

Don't miss

  • Bowland Brewery's beer hall at Holmes Mill, Clitheroe
  • A pint of Moorhouse's in Burnley
  • Hotpot and Lancashire cheese in a Ribble Valley pub
