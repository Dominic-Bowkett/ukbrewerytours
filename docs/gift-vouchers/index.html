FAQ

Gift voucher questions

Do the gift vouchers expire?

No. Our gift vouchers never expire, so the recipient can book whenever suits them.

How is the voucher delivered?

By email, within minutes of purchase. You can send it straight to the recipient (we'll copy you in) or to yourself to hand over. There's no postal option.

Can I print the voucher to give as a present?

Yes. The voucher email links to a printable copy, so you can print it or save it as a PDF to put in a card.

Is it a good last-minute Christmas gift?

Yes. Because vouchers are emailed instantly, you can buy one on Christmas Eve and still have it in time.

Which tours and cities can the voucher be used on?

Any of our brewery tours and beer tastings, in any city we run in. See the full list above.

What if the tour costs more than the voucher?

They can top up the difference. When they redeem, we'll email a secure payment link for the balance.

What happens to leftover credit?

It stays on the voucher code and can be used on a future tour.

How do they redeem it?

They send their voucher code and preferred tour and date through our redemption page, and we confirm the booking by email, usually within 1 working day.

Can I get a refund?

You can request a refund within 24 hours of purchase. After that, vouchers can't be returned, but they never expire. See our returns policy.
