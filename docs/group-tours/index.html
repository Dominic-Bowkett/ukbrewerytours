Private & group tours

Group brewery tours, anywhere in the UK

Corporate teams, clients, stags, hens and big birthdays — private brewery tours and beer tastings from £29 per person, running since 2014.

Where we go

Your city, your group, your tour

We operate in London, Bristol, Manchester, Cardiff, Liverpool, Leeds, Nottingham, Glasgow, Sheffield, Newcastle, Birmingham, Edinburgh, Tunbridge Wells and other locations across the UK.

Every group tour includes beer tastings — typically 1/3 or 1/2 pint measures across a variety of styles — guided visits to multiple breweries, and an experienced brewery tour guide.

Corporate

Team building with proper beer

Our Blindfold Beer Tasting is the ultimate team building beer tasting event for corporate groups. Beyond beer, we can also arrange wine tastings, fitness sessions, farm tours and a whole load of other experiences.

How booking works

Three steps to a great day out

1

Tell us your plans

Email us or use the contact form with your city preference, group size and potential dates.

2

Save the date

A deposit secures your date. The final balance is due at least 7 days before the tour.

3

Meet your guide

Your expert guide takes it from there — breweries, tastings and a route built for your group.

There's no minimum group size, but the typical minimum fee is the equivalent of 10 guests. Deposits are non-refundable unless the tour is cancelled by us or due to government restrictions.
