Tips for a Memorable Brewery Tour in Camden Town

Visiting Camden Town Brewery is a must-do activity for any beer enthusiast. To make the most out of your experience, here are a few tips:

  • Dress comfortably: brewery tours often involve a lot of walking and standing, so make sure to wear comfortable shoes and clothing that allows for freedom of movement.
  • Bring a water bottle: beer tasting can be dehydrating, so it's important to stay hydrated throughout the tour. Bring a water bottle to sip on between tastings.
  • Ask questions: the guides at Camden Town Brewery are knowledgeable and passionate about their craft. Don't be afraid to ask questions and learn as much as you can.
  • Take notes: with so many different beers to taste and breweries to visit, it can be easy to forget what you've learned. Bring a notebook to jot down notes and keep track of your favourites.
  • Engage with the group: part of the fun of a brewery tour is meeting new people who share your passion for beer. Take the opportunity to engage with your fellow tour-mates and make new friends.

By following these tips, you'll be sure to have a memorable and enjoyable experience on your Camden Town Brewery tour.
